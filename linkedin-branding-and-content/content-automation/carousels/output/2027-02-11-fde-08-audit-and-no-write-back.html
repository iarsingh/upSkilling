FDE FIELD NOTES#08

Write-back vs suggest

Write-back
  • Tool updates the record
  • Errors spread downstream
  • Needs change approval
  • Hard to undo
Suggest
  • Tool shows a suggestion
  • Human applies it
  • System of record unchanged
  • Turn off any time

FDE FIELD NOTES#08

An audit log that refuses to store

  1. 01
    IDs, not bodies
    Record which item, not what it said.
  2. 02
    Hashes for proof
    Show the input did not change without keeping it.
  3. 03
    Decision + actor
    Suggestion, final choice, who chose.
  4. 04
    Retention by policy
    Deleted on schedule, provably.

Key takeaway

Suggest first, audit decisions, store nothing you do not need.

  • No write-back until evidence earns it
  • Audit IDs, hashes and decisions
  • Retention is part of the design
