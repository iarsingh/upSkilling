pgvector in practice

  1. 01
    Check the version
    HNSW indexes need pgvector 0.5.0 or later.
  2. 02
    Azure first step
    Allowlist vector in azure.extensions.
  3. 03
    Hybrid search
    Combine full-text and vector scores, then re-rank.
  4. 04
    Filter by tenant
    Permissions in the WHERE clause, not after.

MULTI-CLOUD FDE STACK#06

When pgvector is enough

Stay on Postgres
  • Up to millions of chunks
  • Need joins and filters
  • Customer runs Postgres
  • One fewer security review
Look further
  • Hundreds of millions of vectors
  • Strict latency at high QPS
  • Heavy multi-tenant search
  • Search team already exists
