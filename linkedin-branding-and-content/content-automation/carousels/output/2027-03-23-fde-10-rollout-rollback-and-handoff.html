Scope at handoff

Do
  • ✓Write what is in and out
  • ✓Route new asks to the owner
  • ✓Leave a backlog, not promises
Avoid
  • ✕Quick extra features in week 6
  • ✕Undocumented manual fixes
  • ✕Access that outlives you
