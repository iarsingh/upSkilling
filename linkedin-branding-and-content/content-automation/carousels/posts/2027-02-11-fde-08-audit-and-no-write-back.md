Audit logs and the no-write-back rule.
Suggest first. Write later, if ever.

Two rules saved more pilots than any model choice: the tool does not write back to the system of record, and the audit log stores decisions without storing sensitive content.

The takeaway: Suggest first, audit decisions, store nothing you do not need.
