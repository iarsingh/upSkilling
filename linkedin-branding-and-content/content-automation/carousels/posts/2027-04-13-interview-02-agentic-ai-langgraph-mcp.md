Agentic AI interview questions.
LangGraph, MCP, tool calling and human approval.

Agent questions have moved from “what is an agent” to “how do you stop it doing damage”. Here are the questions I would prepare for, with the answers that show you have shipped one.

Swipe the carousel 👉
1. Questions and short answers
2. A safe tool call
3. The follow-ups
4. How to answer
5. Practise these

The takeaway: The best agent answer starts with the least autonomy that works.

Where do you put the human approval step in your agents?

Or just reply 1, 2 or 3: which is hardest to get your team to do?
1️⃣ Workflow first, agent when needed
2️⃣ Validate and authorise every tool call
3️⃣ Approval for anything that writes
