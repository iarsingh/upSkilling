Identity is the first integration.
IAM, Cloud IAM and Entra ID, without a single static key.

The first week of most deployments is spent waiting for access. Knowing how identity works on each cloud, for people, pods and pipelines, is how you shorten that week and pass the security review.

Swipe the carousel 👉
1. Pods without keys
2. Users: OAuth2, OIDC and JWT
3. RBAC done right
4. Interview scenario
5. Identity checklist

The takeaway: If a credential can be copied, assume it will be.
