Scenario: IT will not approve a third-party model, and the data cannot leave their environment. The VP still wants the night desk helped in two weeks.

The build is a service that reads their export from disk and applies a policy they signed. Every point on the score is visible. Every answer cites the shipment row, the open ticket, and the SOP.

A local model is not the clever workaround. It is still a system the operator cannot recompute, and it spends the two weeks on serving.

Egress is a test, not a promise. No model client in the process, then their IT owner watches one lookup and confirms nothing leaves the VPC.

What would you ship in those two weeks if the model API was banned on day one?
