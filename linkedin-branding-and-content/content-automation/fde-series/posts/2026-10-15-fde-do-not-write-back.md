The lead asks for the score and the recommended SOP to be written into the TMS so the next shift sees them.

That request is reasonable. It is also the moment rollback stops being "turn the service off." A wrong band becomes a field someone will act on, and undoing it is a migration.

Week one stays read-only. The binder is still the path. Writeback waits until the lead agrees on the bands, the old disagreements are rows in the eval file, and a named person is allowed to overwrite that TMS field.

Saving a click is not worth a silent update to the system of record.

What writeback have you postponed, and what evidence would make you allow it?
