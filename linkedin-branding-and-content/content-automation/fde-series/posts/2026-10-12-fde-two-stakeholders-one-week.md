IT wants SSO, and medical shipments restricted to the medical desk, before anyone logs in. The night lead wants the scorer tonight because a grocery load is already late.

Both are right. The sequence is the job.

Tonight can be one read-only lookup, beside the lead, on loads he is already allowed to see. No shared login. No medical row on the grocery desk. SSO and the access rule land before a second person has credentials.

The sponsor who breaks a tie is the VP who owns the outcome, and the decision goes in writing the same day. Helpful access at midnight is how a medical shipment ends up on the wrong screen.

When two customers inside the same account disagree, who do you make decide?
