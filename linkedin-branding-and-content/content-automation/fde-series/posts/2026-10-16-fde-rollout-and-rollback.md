A healthy container is not a successful rollout.

Week one is shadow. The desk still uses the binder. Twenty lookups, under two minutes, and the lead agrees on at least 9 of 10 bands. Week two is one desk, and only the loads the policy was built for.

I stop if an answer has no citation, the eval file fails, the lead overturns more than 1 in 10, or the service calls out of their VPC.

Rollback is stopping the container, and that sentence is only true because nothing was written back to the TMS. Wrong bands with a green health check are still a rollback of the desk.

I am done when their owner can add a shipment to the eval file without me.

What is the stop condition on the last thing you shipped into someone else's environment?
