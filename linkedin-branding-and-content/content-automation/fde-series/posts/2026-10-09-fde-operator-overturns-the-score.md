Scenario: the eval file is green. The night lead reviews 10 shipments and overturns the band on 4.

That is a stop, not a prompt tweak. The rule I would have written before the pilot is simple: more than one overturn in ten and the desk rollout does not expand.

The fix is to sit on those four rows, change a point value only when the lead can say why, and add the row to the eval file. Lowering the threshold until the chart looks calm hides the disagreement. It does not resolve it.

Eval green means the code matches the policy. It does not mean the policy matches the operator.

What is your stop condition when the expert user disagrees?
