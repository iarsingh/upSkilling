# Question-bank content policy

Contribute original explanations and material you have permission to redistribute.
Do not copy paid question banks, confidential employer/customer documents, or interviews
subject to confidentiality obligations. A citation does not itself grant permission to
redistribute a source. Preserve applicable third-party attribution and license notices.

Use organization names only as context; this project is not endorsed by the named
organizations. Label personal responses as templates and never infer employment dates,
compensation, certifications, or achievements from an interview question.

Prefer official technical documentation, include relevant version assumptions, and test
code examples when practical. Automated checks identify some formatting, duplication and
placeholder issues; they do not establish that every answer is technically correct.

For a factual error, open a content-correction issue with the question and supporting
source. For private or confidential information, contact the maintainer through SECURITY.md
without repeating the sensitive content publicly. Maintainers can remove or revise
material whose provenance or suitability cannot be established.
