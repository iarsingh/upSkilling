# Billing fees

Two different jobs live here. One is the ordinary fee table. The other is a waiver when a bank holiday moves the due date. Read the heading before you act.

## Fee schedule

Standard late fees are 1.5 percent of the past-due balance, assessed on the fifth calendar day after the due date. The schedule does not authorize a waiver. Agents who only need the percent should stop at this section.

## Bank holiday waiver

When the contractual due date falls on a bank holiday, waive the late fee. In the billing console choose Waive, reason HOLIDAY-DUE-DATE, and attach the holiday calendar link for that year. The waiver covers the fee that would have posted because the payment window moved. It does not forgive the underlying balance. A fee assessed for an ordinary late payment, with no holiday involved, stays in place.
