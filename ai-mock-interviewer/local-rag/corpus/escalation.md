# After-hours escalation

Page the network operations center when a customer-facing incident is still open and the local on-call has not acknowledged it within 15 minutes.

## Bridge

The after-hours bridge is +1-555-014-1900, conference code 1900. The path was last confirmed in March 2026. Join the bridge, state the customer, the impact, and the incident channel, then wait for the NOC lead to assign an incident commander. Do not invent a different number from memory. This page is the source for the after-hours path.

## Who to page

Page the NOC primary first. If there is no answer in 10 minutes, page the NOC secondary. Engineering managers are not the after-hours front door unless the NOC lead asks for them.
