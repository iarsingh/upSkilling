# Visitor badges and parking

Facilities issues badges at the lobby desk. This page is not a payments, refund, or on-call procedure.

## Badge color

A visitor badge is blue. Employees wear gray. Contractors wear yellow. A blue badge expires the same day at 18:00 local time and does not open the packing closet or the payments room. Lost blue badges are reprinted at the lobby desk. Do not page the network operations center for a badge problem.

## Parking

Visitors park in lot C and bring the ticket to the lobby for validation. Lot C is not related to card captures, disk maintenance, or warehouse printers.
