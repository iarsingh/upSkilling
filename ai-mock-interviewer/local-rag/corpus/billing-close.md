# Monthly close statement delays

The finance close job finishes at 02:00 local time on the first business day after the period ends. Customer-facing statements are generated from that job.

## Statements after the close

Charges that post in the last hour of the period can miss the close job. Those charges show on the statement generated the next morning, so the statement a customer opens at period end can look short. This is expected. It is not a failed payment and it is not a gateway timeout. Support should tell the customer the remaining charges appear on the next statement run, and should quote the close time of 02:00. Do not refund those charges. Do not call this document a payment failure.

## What this page does not use

This procedure never names a commercial document type other than "statement." Searchers who say "bill," "receipt," or a synonym should still land here when they mean the monthly close delay.
